CIRCULAR ECONOMY

A circular economy keeps materials moving instead of treating them as disposable.

Instead of taking something from a property and sending it straight to the landfill, a circular approach looks for the next useful life. An item can be recovered, repaired, restored, resold, reused, or broken down into materials that can be repurposed again.
