Circular Network helps identify, recover, and redirect usable materials before they become waste.

WHAT WE DO

Recovery-first support for transitions, cleanouts, and property changes.

Property & Real Estate Cleanouts

Recover usable furniture, décor, appliances, building materials, and household goods during property transitions.

Estate Transitions

Sort and identify items that may have continued value rather than automatically sending everything to disposal.

Staging & Property Removal

Help recover furnishings, décor, and other materials removed during staging changes and property preparation.

Property Management Cleanouts

Create a recovery-first approach to clearing units and properties.

Materials Sorting & Recovery

Separate reusable, recyclable, salvageable, and disposal-bound materials.

Resale & Reuse

Give recovered items another pathway through resale, donation, reuse, or repurposing.
